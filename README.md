# FEngine — Financial & Economic Analysis Engine

FEngine is an extensible, evidence-driven catalog of financial and economic metrics, analytical formulas and methodologies for enterprises and organizations across sectors.

## Mission
Turn source facts into reproducible indicators, versioned formulas, multi-metric analyses, and traceable analytical conclusions.

## Boundaries
- **Fact**: sourced, typed observation (stock/flow, currency, period, unit, dimensions, provenance).
- **Metric**: a stable, uniquely coded business meaning.
- **Formula version**: immutable expression, inputs, units, conditions, aggregation and zero-denominator policy.
- **Methodology version**: curated collection of metrics, formulas, decision rules and applicability constraints.
- **Analysis**: executed, reproducible use of an admitted methodology for a specific organization, period and dataset.
- **Sector profile**: bank, insurer, industrial, trade, SaaS, public, nonprofit, etc.

## Delivery
Initial work is specification-first: catalog, domain taxonomy, formulas, calculation contracts, validation and test fixtures. No indicator is treated as financially qualified until its methodology, assumptions and reference examples are approved.

The active working branch for initial catalog development will be `feat/fengine-metric-methodology-foundation`, branching from `main`.
